SGTM Automix

SGTM Automix is a gain-sharing automixer for speech. Put one instance on
every talker's track; the instances find each other and keep the total gain
at one open microphone, so whoever is speaking comes up and the others duck.

This installer places two plugins in your personal plug-in folders:

  * Audio Unit (AU)   ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/SGTM Automix.component
  * VST3              ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/SGTM Automix.vst3

Both install into your own home folder, so no administrator password is
needed and nothing is changed system-wide. You can deselect either format on
the next screen if you only need one.

Requirements: macOS 13 or newer, on Apple Silicon or Intel.


Signing

This installer is signed with an Apple Developer ID (Sounds Good To Me AS)
and notarized by Apple, as are both plugins it installs. Nothing needs to be
approved in System Settings and there is no Terminal step.


After installing

Restart your DAW, or trigger a plugin rescan, so it picks up the new plugin.
Insert SGTM Automix on each speech track, after EQ and before any
compressor.
